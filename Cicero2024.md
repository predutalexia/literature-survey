# Cicero2024: Simulation of Quantum Computers: Review and Acceleration Opportunities

- **Type:** Review (preprint)
- **Priority:** Primary
- **Access:** Open access
- **Reference:** `references.bib`, key `Cicero2024`
- **Original:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12660

## Summary

Chalmers review (v2, March 2025) of the levels at which quantum computers are simulated and of
acceleration approaches on CPU, GPU and FPGA, including algorithmic and hardware-aware optimisations
and future directions.

## Use in my thesis

The best single overview for the state-of-the-art chapter from a computer-architecture angle. Use it
to list existing GPU simulators and the common optimisations (gate fusion, cache blocking, etc.) and
to position my simple CUDA simulator relative to them.

## Gap

Survey; gives no from-scratch implementation.

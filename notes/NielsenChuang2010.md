## Summary

The standard textbook. Defines qubits, the state vector, unitary gates (X, H, CNOT, Toffoli),
measurement and the circuit model.

## Use in my thesis

Source for every definition in my theory chapter: qubit, tensor product, the gate matrices for X, H
and CNOT, measurement probabilities |a_i|^2. Cite it rather than tutorials for definitions.

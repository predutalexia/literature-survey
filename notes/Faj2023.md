# Faj2023: Quantum Computer Simulations at Warp Speed: Assessing the Impact of GPU Acceleration

- **Type:** Conference paper
- **Priority:** Primary
- **Access:** Not open access
- **Reference:** `references.bib`, key `Faj2023`
- **Original:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.14860

## Summary

Evaluates Qiskit Aer's GPU backends (Thrust and cuQuantum) on NVIDIA A100s with a benchmark suite.
For many qubits the GPU backends reach up to 14x over the CPU backend, cuQuantum adds 1.5-3x over
Thrust, and on multi-GPU the host-device data movement is the limiting factor.

## Use in my thesis

Methodology template for my CPU vs GPU chapter (which circuits, which metrics, how to report speed-
up) and a published speed-up range to compare my results against. Its finding on host-device
transfer tells me to time kernels separately from memory copies.

## Gap

Evaluates existing simulators, does not build one.
